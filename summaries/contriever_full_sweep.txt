contriever_full_sweep (2026-07-25)

IDEA / PARAMETERS: Generalization test for all three headline findings, which until now rested entirely on BM25 - a single sparse lexical retriever. Contriever (dense semantic, facebook/contriever, 110M params) was chosen as the maximally contrastive paradigm: if the conclusions hold on both ends of the sparse-lexical/dense-semantic spectrum, they hold across retrieval paradigms. A secondary hope - that dense retrieval pools would be lexically more varied and so widen the weak lambda->ILD manipulation range from the BM25 sweep - was also part of the motivation. Design: exact replication of the full BM25 coverage on the new ranker (deterministic + MMR lambda in {0.15,0.3,0.45,0.55,0.7,0.85,1.0} + PL alpha in {1,2,4,8} at N=10, all 7 LaMP tasks, nq=100, seed=42) = 84 new runs, 31,248 generation units. Retrieval precompute (~50 min, all 7 tasks verified at exact expected query counts) plus a 224.5-minute experiment run, both deliberately on Balance Mode: the chassis thermally saturated on Performance Mode mid-precompute (87C, software throttling active at ~117W), and Balance's ~80W turned out to be roughly the chassis's sustainable dissipation envelope anyway, making the sustained-run throughput cost modest (a policy now recorded in the gpu-power-profile memory). One flaky-network lesson reapplied: launched with HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 from the start. Analysis script made ranker-aware (per-ranker sections, pooled interaction re-fit).

RESULTS / CONCLUSIONS: The lambda=1.0 sanity check passed exactly on all 7 tasks on the new ranker (EU and ILD identical to deterministic to 6 decimals). The secondary hypothesis failed informatively: ILD manipulation ranges are nearly identical to BM25's (0.02-0.08 on six tasks, 0.23 on LaMP-6), because LaMP reranks one user's own profile documents - pool homogeneity is intrinsic to the benchmark, not the retriever, so widening the manipulation needs a different corpus, not a different ranker. The generalization verdict: all three headline findings survive. (1) Fairness stays utility-neutral (pooled two-ranker EE-D coefficient +0.023, p=0.051, n=13,228). (2) At matched diversity, PL still adds nothing over MMR (per-task weighted deltas +0.016/-0.013/+0.013/+0.006, sign-mixed, tiny). (3) The task-dependent diversity pattern replicates - LaMP-2's positive diversity effect strengthens to significance (+2.66, p=0.026; raw EU 0.17->0.22 as lambda drops), generation tasks stay flat-to-negative - while BM25's pooled negative ILD coefficient does NOT replicate on Contriever (-0.072, p=0.25 vs -0.247, p=1.7e-05), confirming it was substantially cross-task composition rather than a uniform law. The EE-D x ILD interaction graduated from lead to robust finding: p=0.00038 on n=13,228 (from p=0.050 at n=3.5k and p=0.049 at n=7.1k), same coherent shape throughout (less-fair rankings trend better at low diversity; effect cancels near ILD=1 where most data sits) - though R^2 stays ~0.003, so it is a real but practically tiny second-order structure and should be framed as such.
