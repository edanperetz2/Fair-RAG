t5base_full_grid (2026-07-25 to 2026-07-26)

IDEA / PARAMETERS: The final experiment of the project - a generator-axis validation completing a full 2x2 grid (generator size x retrieval paradigm). Motivation: the stats notebook had flagged that even gold-oracle retrieval yields low EU on some tasks with flanT5Small, raising a floor-effect threat - a too-weak generator compresses every reranking effect toward zero, so the headline "fairness is utility-neutral" could be an artifact of the generator having no headroom. flanT5Base (250M, ~3.2x Small) was the right instrument because the benchmark's utility labels are generator-specific and prebuilt sets exist only for Small/Base/XXL (T5-large would need a multi-day label-generation pipeline; XXL does not fit an 8GB GPU). Design: exact replication of the consolidated Small suite on Base - deterministic + MMR lambda in {0.15,0.3,0.45,0.55,0.7,0.85,1.0} + PL alpha in {1,2,4,8} at N=10, all 7 LaMP tasks, nq=100, seed=42, BOTH BM25 and Contriever = 168 configs, 67,200 generation units. Ran 571 minutes overnight on the Balanced hardware profile with zero failures. Operational notes: the first launch attempt via Windows scheduled task was silently defeated by an unnoticed charger disconnection (schtasks defaults to "start only on AC" and "stop on battery switch"; the battery then drained to critical sleep) - reconstructed afterwards from the Kernel-Power event log; a mobile push-notification channel and an AC-power watcher were added mid-session so power loss now alerts the user's phone within a minute; and an HF cache layout mismatch (HF_HOME hub/ layout vs PromptLM's cache_dir layout) was diagnosed when the smoke test failed offline.

RESULTS / CONCLUSIONS: The lambda=1.0 sanity check passed exactly on all 14 (ranker, task) pairs. The floor effect was real: Base's deterministic EU is far higher than Small's (LaMP-1: 0.72 vs 0.12; LaMP-3: 0.67 vs 0.46). With that headroom, the story changed in important ways. Generator-ROBUST (all four cells of the grid): PL adds approximately nothing over MMR at matched diversity (per-task weighted deltas -0.03..+0.03, sign-mixed everywhere) - the proposal's core answer, "fair reranking's only active ingredient is its diversity side-effect," is the project's most solid conclusion. Generator-DEPENDENT: (1) diversity's within-MMR effect FLIPPED SIGN - negative/ns on Small (BM25 -0.237; Contriever -0.072) but positive and significant on both Base cells (BM25 +0.126, p=0.012; Contriever +0.214, p=7e-05) - the stronger generator exploits diverse contexts that confused the weaker one; (2) a real fairness-utility tradeoff emerged on Base (EE-D main effect +0.143, p=6e-21, vs ~0 on Small) - in this replication the tradeoff the original paper argued against is generator-dependent, invisible at 77M and measurable at 250M, though the matched-diversity net cost remains small; (3) the EE-D x ILD interaction that had "graduated" to p=0.0004 on Small did NOT replicate on Base (p=0.65) - it was generator-specific structure, vindicating the lead-grade framing. Final empirical position: diversity is the mechanism, its sign depends on generator capacity, and the fairness cost grows with generator strength - whether it keeps growing (XXL-class) is the report's future-work question.
