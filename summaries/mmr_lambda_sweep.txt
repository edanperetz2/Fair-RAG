mmr_lambda_sweep (2026-07-25)

IDEA / PARAMETERS: The direct mechanism test the two previous batches couldn't provide. In all Plackett-Luce data, diversity (ILD) and fairness (EE-D) co-vary through alpha, so their effects on utility can never be fully separated using PL runs alone. MMR breaks that confound: it is deterministic (EE-D pinned at 1.0 for every lambda), and its lambda parameter is a pure diversity dial. Sweeping lambda in {0.15, 0.3, 0.45, 0.7, 0.85, 1.0} (six new points around the existing 0.55) across all 7 LaMP tasks - BM25/flanT5Small/nq=100/seed=42 unchanged for direct comparability - gives experimentally controlled variation in ILD at perfectly fixed fairness. Lambda=1.0 doubles as a built-in sanity check, since pure-relevance MMR must reproduce the deterministic ranking. 42 new runs (91 total in experiment_runs/), completed in 28.9 minutes on fresh-GPU rates after two launch notes: the power scheme had reverted to the throttling Legion Balance Mode (caught in pre-flight, switched before launch), and flaky HuggingFace network checks stalled the first launch's model loads (fixed by relaunching with HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1; model fully cached locally). Analysis in experiments/analyze_mmr_sweep.py; select_best_precision() moved into the shared analysis package as part of this work.

RESULTS / CONCLUSIONS: The sanity check passed exactly - lambda=1.0 matches the deterministic run's EU and ILD to six decimals on all 7 tasks. The manipulation itself proved weaker than hoped on most tasks (ILD ranges of just 0.01-0.09 for six tasks; 0.25 on LaMP-6) because BM25's candidate pools are already lexically similar, so per-task regressions are underpowered. The headline results: (1) the pooled within-MMR EU~ILD coefficient is -0.247 (p=1.7e-05, n=4,144), replicating the earlier negative diversity effect with fairness held perfectly fixed - so that result was not a PL-randomization confound - but per-task the picture is heterogeneous and partly compositional: diversity HELPS the classification tasks (LaMP-2's EU rises 0.10 to 0.17 from lambda=1.0 to 0.15; LaMP-3 from 0.46 to ~0.55) and mildly hurts generation tasks (LaMP-7 significant negative). (2) The matched-diversity test - PL vs MMR utility compared within ILD quantile bins - shows deltas of -0.044 to +0.020 with no consistent sign: once diversity is matched, randomization-based fair reranking adds approximately nothing over explicit-diversity reranking. This is the most direct answer to the proposal's research question so far: fairness has no direct utility effect; what movement exists is attributable to the diversity side-effect, whose sign is task-dependent, not a uniform benefit. (3) The EE-D x ILD interaction lead from the alpha sweep re-fit on the doubled dataset (n=7,104) lands at p=0.049 with the same coherent pattern (less-fair rankings trend better at low diversity, effect cancels near ILD~1 where most data lives) but R^2 stays ~0.006 - still a lead, not a conclusion.
